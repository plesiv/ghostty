Why Ghostty

All the things you want.
None of the trade-offs.

A terminal shouldn’t make you choose between performance, features, and a great platform experience. Ghostty brings them together.

↯

Seriously fast

A carefully tuned, multithreaded architecture keeps input responsive and rendering smooth—even when the terminal is working hard.

⌘

Feels at home

SwiftUI and Metal on macOS; GTK on Linux. Ghostty embraces platform conventions instead of settling for one-size-fits-all.

▤

Rich terminal features

Standards-aware emulation with modern protocols, plus native windows, tabs, splits, and the details that make workflows yours.
